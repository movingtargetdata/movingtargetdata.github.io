Accepted Tutorial @ AAAI'27

The Moving Target of Data Curation

Data selection and data synthesis are two approaches to the same objective: maximizing the informativeness of the data models learn from.

AboutOutlineOrganizersStay updated

About

A myriad of works have shown that the data ingested by models is the ultimate driver of model capability and reliability. Still, data curation is scattered across disparate subfields such as data efficiency and synthetic data generation. This tutorial brings these areas together and presents them in a unified light.

It is meant as an introduction to data curation research for newcomers, a refresher on newer topics for practitioners, and a foundational discussion for data curation experts.

Outline

  1. Why data curation is still a problemHow the demands on what models should learn from data keep changing.
  2. Curation across the training stagesHow curation differs in pre/mid-training, supervised fine-tuning, and post-training / RLVR.
  3. Techniques, old and newClassic methods that are still relevant, and state-of-the-art approaches to data selection and synthesis.
  4. Open problemsDirections for future research.
